Goal Live Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026 · App published by Tumia

Lire cette politique en français

Goal Live is a free app for live football scores, fixtures, standings, player and team pages, and news. This page explains what data is processed, why, and what your rights are.

1. No account required

Goal Live works without creating an account. The app does not ask for your name, phone number or location. Your favourites (matches, teams, players, competitions), preferences (language, theme, notifications), recent searches, the “24 hours without ads” period and a copy of the latest data you viewed (so the app works on a slow connection) are stored only on your phone. They are deleted if you uninstall the app.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is funded by advertising from Google AdMob (banners, ads inside lists labelled “Ad”, full-screen ads, and an optional rewarded video). To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, technical information (model, Android version, language, approximate IP address) and your interactions with ads.

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and other regions where it is required, your consent is requested (Google's form). You can change it at any time in the app: Settings → Privacy options. Without consent, ads are not personalised.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads / Privacy).
  • The “Remove ads for 24 h” video is optional: you choose to watch it.
  • Learn more: how Google uses information from partner apps.

3. Goal Live server (Supabase)

Scores, matches, profiles and news are provided by our server, hosted by Supabase in the European Union (Stockholm, Sweden). Like any web server, it temporarily keeps technical logs (IP address, date, requested data) for security and abuse prevention. These logs are not used to identify you and are not sold.

4. “Contact us” form

If you write to us from the app, we receive the name, e-mail address and message you enter. They are stored on our server (Supabase) and sent by e-mail through the Resend service, only to reply to you. They are deleted on request, and at the latest 24 months after the last exchange.

5. Match share links

When you share a match, the link only contains the match number. If someone installs Goal Live from that link, the app reads the install information provided by Google Play (“Play Install Referrer”) once, to open the shared match directly. No personal data is attached to it.

6. Sports data and news

Match, player and team data come from API-Football; news comes from public RSS feeds of sports media. No personal data is sent to these providers: requests go through our server. When you open an article on a media website, that website applies its own policy.

7. Sharing and sale

We never sell personal data. Data is only shared with the service providers needed to run the app listed above (Google AdMob, Supabase, Resend).

8. Security

Communication between the app and our server is encrypted (HTTPS). Access to the server is restricted and protected.

9. Children

Goal Live is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Your rights

You can request access to, correction or deletion of data about you (for example a message sent through the form), or object to its processing, by writing to the address below. If you live in the European Union, you can also lodge a complaint with your country's data protection authority.
